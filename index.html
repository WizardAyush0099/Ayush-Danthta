02 — Studio

Atmosphere,
engineered.

A one-person studio for the in-between work: art direction, interface and the code that makes both feel inevitable.

I build websites that feel like rooms — then make them load in under a second.

I’m Ayush Danthta, a designer and developer working from Shimla, India. Most projects start with a feeling — a restaurant at dusk, a product that needs to feel trustworthy, a study tool that shouldn’t feel like homework — and end in a page that holds that feeling without a single ornament doing nothing.

The range is deliberate. Product surfaces like Study Hub sit next to single-purpose sites for cafés, studios and labels. Different scales, same standard: a clear point of view, motion with a reason, and performance treated as part of the design.

04 — Case study

One hub,
four doors.

The project that proves the range: a single product shell holding three very different experiences.

Study Hub — 2026

Learning without
the homework feel.

Study Hub is a learning platform with three distinct doors: a music-theory lab, a training programme and a small e-book store. The brief was to make them feel like one place without flattening them into the same page.

The shell carries the navigation, type system and pacing; each section gets its own accent, density and interaction model. Commerce stays quiet until it is needed. Lessons lead with rhythm and motion rather than walls of text.

07 — Process

A short,
visible process.

No black boxes. You see the frame, the design and the build as they happen.

Four beats. No surprises.

Every project starts with a written frame and ends with source you own. The preview link is live from the first week, so feedback happens on the real thing rather than on a picture of it.

01

Frame

One call, one paragraph: who it’s for, what it must feel like, what “done” means. I write the brief back to you so we agree on the room before we decorate it.

Day 1 — 2
02

Direction

Type, palette and one motif, shown as a full-height composition rather than a moodboard. Two rounds of change are included before the build starts.

Week 1
03

Build

The page is assembled live and shared as a preview link. Motion, responsive rules and performance are handled in the same pass as the layout.

Week 2 — 3
04

Ship

Deploy, hand over the source and a one-page guide to editing copy. One week of fixes after launch, then it’s yours.
